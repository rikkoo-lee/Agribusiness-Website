About Us

Agribusiness How It Works is dedicated to helping Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and their families build a sustainable future back home.

Founded by Buddy Gancenia, our mission is guided by a simple but powerful principle: "Instruct. Inspire. Succeed." We believe that farming and agribusiness are the keys to reuniting families. By providing accessible, high-quality video tutorials on livestock, crops, and aquaculture, we empower Filipinos to start their own agricultural businesses and retire comfortably in the Philippines.

Whether you are a beginner looking to plant your first seed or an experienced farmer wanting to scale up, we are here to guide you every step of the way.
